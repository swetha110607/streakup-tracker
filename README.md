📌 About the Project
StreakUp is a full-stack habit and progress tracking web app built for students and learners who want to stay consistent and accountable. It started as a personal tool to track DSA learning alongside friends, and grew into a multi-habit tracker that works for anything — coding, reading, exercise, meditation, and more.
The core idea: log what you do every day, visualise your progress over time, and never break your streak.

✨ Features

🎯 Multi-habit tracking — 11 built-in habits including DSA & Coding, Reading, Exercise, Meditation, Water Intake, Sleep, and more
✏️ Custom habits — add your own habit with AI-generated relevant form fields based on context
📊 Dynamic progress charts — per-habit bar charts showing the last 7 days of progress using Recharts
🗓️ Streak calendar — monthly calendar view showing completed, missed, and today's entries per habit
🔥 Daily streaks — streak counter per habit to keep you motivated and consistent
📝 Smart notes — log key points, formulas, algorithms, or takeaways per session
🌙 Light and dark mode — toggle from the navbar or Profile page, preference saved across sessions
👤 Profile page — edit your name, pick an avatar, and manage appearance settings
🔒 Anonymous auth — no email or password needed, just enter your name and start tracking

💡 How It Works

Enter your name on the landing screen — no email or password required
Pick a habit from the dropdown (DSA, Reading, Exercise, or add your own)
Log your daily progress — the form adapts to your chosen habit
View your progress on the Dashboard — charts and streak calendar update per habit
Save key notes and takeaways from each session
Stay consistent and watch your streaks grow 🔥
